Privacy Policy

Photography Booking Manager

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Information We Use

The app may handle booking information such as customer names, mobile numbers, event dates, event types, venue details and payment amounts entered by the app user.

2. How Information Is Stored

Booking information is intended to be stored in the Google Sheets spreadsheet selected by the app owner. The app may also store booking information locally on the user's device to support offline access and later synchronisation.

3. Google Account Access

The app uses Google authorisation to access the Google Sheets data permitted by the user. Google account access is used only for the app's stated booking-management purpose.

4. Sharing of Information

The app developer does not intend to sell or rent booking information to third parties. Information may be processed by Google services according to Google's privacy policies.

5. Data Security

Users should protect their Google account and devices. They should not share access to their booking spreadsheet with unauthorised people.

6. Data Deletion

The app user can manage or delete booking records directly from their Google Sheets spreadsheet and clear locally stored app data from their device.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact email provided in the app's Google consent screen.
